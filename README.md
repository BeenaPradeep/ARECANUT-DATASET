# Arecanut Disease Detection Dataset – 6 Classes

## Overview

This repository contains a field-image dataset developed for research on automated disease detection and classification in arecanut (*Areca catechu* L.). The dataset comprises images of healthy and diseased arecanut leaves and nuts collected from arecanut plantations in Chikkamagaluru district, Karnataka, India.

The dataset is intended to support research in computer vision, machine learning, deep learning, and automated plant disease detection.

## Dataset Classes

The dataset contains six classes:

1. Bud Rot
2. Healthy Leaf
3. Healthy Nut
4. Koleroga
5. Ring Spot
6. Yellow Leaf

## Dataset Organization

The images are organized into six class-specific folders:

- `BUD ROT`
- `HEALTHY LEAF`
- `HEALTHY NUT`
- `KOLEROGA`
- `RING SPOT`
- `YELLOW LEAF`

Each folder contains images corresponding to the respective class.

## Data Collection

The images were collected under natural field conditions from arecanut plantations in Karnataka, India. The field images represent healthy and diseased conditions of arecanut leaves and nuts.

Images captured under field conditions may contain variations in illumination, background, plant orientation, disease severity, and viewing angle. These variations are intended to provide realistic samples for developing and evaluating automated disease detection models.

## Image Annotation

The images were manually reviewed and categorized according to their corresponding health or disease condition.

Farmers with practical knowledge of arecanut cultivation were consulted as agricultural domain experts to assist in the identification of disease symptoms and categorization of the images. Their contribution was limited to providing agricultural expertise for image annotation. No personal, identifiable, or sensitive information was collected from the individuals involved in the annotation process.

## Intended Use

The dataset may be used for research and educational purposes, including:

- Arecanut disease detection
- Arecanut disease classification
- Plant disease image classification
- Convolutional neural network (CNN) research
- Deep learning and machine learning research
- Computer vision applications
- Development and evaluation of automated agricultural disease detection systems

## Ethical Considerations

This dataset does not contain human-subject experimental data. Farmers were consulted solely as agricultural domain experts for disease identification and image annotation. Their involvement did not include human-subject experimentation or intervention, and no personal or sensitive information was collected.
